Add a job location and type (sealcoat, crack fill, striping, paving) to calculate surface temp, cure viability, and rain buffer windows.

3. Smart Alerts

Retention Engine

Receive risk alerts when forecasts shift: rain moves earlier, cure threshold drops, or planned jobs become unsafe.

4. Contractor Metrics

Your Operational Moat

Track air/surface temperature, dew point spread, humidity, rain timing, wind, UV, and cloud cover with derived cure-risk logic.

Simple Action Signal

Each job/day receives a score: 90-100 excellent, 70-89 caution, below 70 not recommended.

When weather turns against a planned job, suggest next-best days and support auto-reschedule workflows.
